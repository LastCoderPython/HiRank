# Design System: RankRoute

## Brand Identity
- **Brand Name:** RankRoute
- **Vibe:** Trustworthy, modern academic advisor, sleek AI technology.

## Typography
- **Font Family:** 'Inter', sans-serif (Primary) or 'Plus Jakarta Sans'
- **Scale:** High-contrast headings, clean body copy.

## Color Palette
- **Primary Background (Light Mode):** #F8F9FA (Clean Off-White)
- **Primary Background (Dark Mode):** #121212 to #1E1E24 (Slate Grey/Midnight)
- **Sidebar/Surface:** #1A237E (Deep Indigo)
- **Accent Color:** #00E5FF (Electric Cyan) or #FF6D00 (Vibrant Orange)
- **Text:** #1A237E (Deep Indigo for headings)

## Components
- **Buttons:** Large, prominent, tech-forward styling.
- **Visual Effects:** Subtle blurred abstract gradients of Deep Indigo and Electric Cyan.
